WILLOW PARK CHECKS — ONEDRIVE TABLET SETUP

1. Open https://willowparkmontessori.github.io/WillowParkChecks/
2. Install / Add to Home screen if required.
3. Open Settings.
4. Add staff members if needed.
5. Confirm Academic year and Current term.
6. Under Microsoft OneDrive, tap Connect OneDrive.
7. Sign in with the Willow Park Microsoft account and approve the requested app-folder access if prompted.
8. Return to Settings and confirm the status says Connected.
9. Complete one TEST assessment.
10. Confirm the completion screen says "PDF backed up to Willow Park OneDrive".
11. On the office computer, open OneDrive and check:
    Apps > Willow Park Checks > Risk Assessments > [Academic Year] > [Term] > [Area]
12. Open the PDF and confirm it is readable and correctly formatted.

The Microsoft password is entered only on Microsoft's own sign-in page. No password or client secret belongs in the GitHub repository.

If the tablet is offline or Microsoft sign-in needs renewing, the assessment remains saved locally and the PDF upload can be retried later.
