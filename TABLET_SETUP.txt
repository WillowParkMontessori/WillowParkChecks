WILLOW PARK CHECKS V2.5 — SAMSUNG TABLET SETUP

1. Open the live Willow Park Checks GitHub Pages site.
2. Install/Add to Home screen if required.
3. Open Settings.
4. Add staff members if needed.
5. Under PDF cloud archive, enter the Willow Park cloud account email and password.
6. Tap Connect this tablet.
7. The status should show Connected.
8. Complete one TEST assessment.
9. Confirm the completion screen says PDF backed up to Willow Park cloud.
10. On the office computer, open Supabase → Storage → risk-assessments and confirm the PDF is present and readable.

The tablet remains signed in between sessions. Do not place the account password, database password, or any Supabase secret/service-role key in GitHub.
